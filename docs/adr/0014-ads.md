# 0014 — Ads: generic slot, AdSense first, never during play

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-03

## Context and Problem Statement

Revenue comes from ads, but ads must not harm gameplay or new-player conversion.

## Decision Outcome

- Build a generic ad-slot component; start with AdSense or no ads at launch; switch to a games-focused network (e.g. AdinPlay, Playwire) once traffic qualifies.
- Placements: pause screen (non-blocking, never covering controls or chat) and a banner on the sign-in screen.
- **Never during play.** No full-screen ads after sign-in.
- Requires a consent banner (Google's free CMP available with AdSense).

### Consequences

- Good: gameplay unaffected; network can be swapped.
- Bad: revenue is negligible at launch scale.
